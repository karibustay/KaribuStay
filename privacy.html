Privacy Policy

How Nerava handles the information needed to help guests find stays and hosts manage their properties.

Effective date: 29 August 2026. This policy should be reviewed by Nerava's legal representative before public launch, especially if payment, location, messaging, advertising, or analytics features are added.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating or using an account, including your name, email address, phone number, profile information, listing details, booking requests, and support messages. We also keep operational records needed to maintain your account, prevent misuse, and support bookings.

How we use information

We use this information to provide Nerava, verify hosts and listings, respond to support requests, manage bookings, improve safety, and communicate essential service updates. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

When information is shared

When a guest sends a booking request, the relevant details are shared with the host so the host can respond. Hosts may share listing information with guests through the service. We also use service providers that help us run the platform, including authentication, database, and file-storage providers. They may process data only to provide their services to Nerava.

Data retention and security

We keep account and booking information while an account is active, and afterwards only as needed for legitimate business, safety, dispute, and legal purposes. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can update your account details in Nerava. To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your identity and retain limited information where required for safety, fraud prevention, disputes, or law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be available at this page, with its effective date shown above.

Privacy contact

Email info@karibustay.com with the subject “Nerava privacy request”. You can also review our Terms of Use.
