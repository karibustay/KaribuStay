Who We Are

A Tanzanian platform built for Tanzanians — and the world.

Nerava started with a simple observation: finding a good room or apartment should be easier than it is today.

Word of mouth, unreliable listings, and no guarantee of quality left guests frustrated and property owners underserved. We set out to change that.

Nerava is a trusted marketplace connecting guests with verified apartments, hotel rooms, and short stays — with transparent pricing and real support.

The name says it all. Karibu means “welcome” in Swahili — and that warmth is at the heart of everything we do.

Our Mission

To make quality accommodation accessible, trustworthy, and easy to find for every guest visiting or moving through Tanzania.

Our Vision

To become the go-to platform for accommodation across East Africa — starting right here in Tanzania.
